Master's thesis

Submitted in October 2026 at RWTH Aachen University. The defence is still to come.

Fundamental Data Integration for Natural Gas Price Range Forecasting: A Machine Learning Approach

Can supply and demand data (gas storage, weather, production, LNG) forecast US natural gas prices better than past prices alone? Instead of one number, my models forecast a range, so a decision-maker sees the likely price and the risk around it.

  • Built a data pipeline from five public sources, aligning each data point to the date it was actually published, so the model never sees the future.
  • Forecast low, middle and high prices with XGBoost quantile regression, then calibrated the ranges with conformal prediction.
  • Compared against classic baselines (random walk, ARIMA, GARCH) with walk-forward testing.
